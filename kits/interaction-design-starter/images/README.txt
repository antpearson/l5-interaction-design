Put your own images in this folder.

Before you do:
  - Resize them. A 6000px photo straight off a camera will destroy your scroll
    performance. 2000px on the long edge is plenty for full-width.
  - Export as WebP or JPEG, not PNG, unless you need transparency.
  - Note where each one came from and under what licence. You will need that
    for the credits block and for Component 1.
