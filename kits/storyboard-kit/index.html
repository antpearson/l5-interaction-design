The notation

  • The viewport — what is on screen at this moment.
  • Held in place while the reader scrolls → position: sticky or a pin.
  • Moves once, in this direction, when triggered → gsap.from().
  • Tied to the scrollbar; reverses on the way back up → scrub, Week 7.
  • The trigger point — where in the viewport this fires → start: 'top 80%'.
  • The reader can act here — your designed moment of interaction.
  • The reduced-motion version of this frame → gsap.matchMedia().
