Interaction Design · Week 4

Storyboard kit

One frame per beat, one for each row of your beat sheet. Draw the frame, mark what is held and what moves, fill in the five notes, and write one line on why. This key is page one; the eighteen frames are on the three pages after it. Print the frame pages again if your piece has more beats. There is a filled-in example too.

The notation

  • The viewport: what is on screen at this beat.
  • Held in place while the reader scrolls.
  • Moves once, in this direction, when triggered.
  • Tied to the scrollbar; runs backwards when the reader scrolls back.
  • The trigger point: where in the viewport this fires.
  • The reader can act here: your designed moment of interaction.
