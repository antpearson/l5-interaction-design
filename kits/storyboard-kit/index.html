Storyboard kit

Print a few of these. Pages two onwards are blank frames: you will want more than four.

The notation

  • The viewport: what is on screen at this beat.
  • Held in place while the reader scrolls.
  • Moves once, in this direction, when triggered.
  • Tied to the scrollbar; runs backwards when the reader scrolls back.
  • The trigger point: where in the viewport this fires.
  • The reader can act here: your designed moment of interaction.

A beat is one thing that happens as the reader scrolls: a moment where the page changes state. One row per beat. The test for a row: “After this, the reader knows or feels ______.” If the answer repeats the row above, merge them.

Storyboard frames

One frame per beat. Draw the frame, mark what is held, mark what changes, fill the notes, and write one line on why this change serves the story.
