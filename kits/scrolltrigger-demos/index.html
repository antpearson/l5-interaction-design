ScrollTrigger demos

Four demos with the markers on. Open one, scroll slowly, and watch where the markers meet.

  1. Arrive: start: "top 80%". The heading plays on its own once its top edge reaches 80% down the screen.
  2. Scrub: start: "top 80%", end: "top 40%", scrub: true. Your scrolling drives the heading between the two.
  3. Try the values: change both values of start and end and watch the markers move.
  4. Held picture: the Grand Hotel on Granby Street, Leicester, in 2009 and about 1903. One trigger per beat, each changing something in the held photo: a swap, stagger, a slide in, and a timeline. Scroll back up and toggleActions undoes each one.

Each demo is a folder laid out like your own project: index.html, css/style.css, js/main.js and, where it needs them, images. Open its index.html in a browser and read the three files in VS Code.

They need an internet connection: GSAP loads from jsDelivr.
