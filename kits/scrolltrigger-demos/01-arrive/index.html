Each heading has a dashed outline so you can see its top edge. Until it starts, the heading is invisible: its start marker shows where it is. The green start marker sits on that top edge and scrolls up with the heading. The green scroller-start line sits 80% of the way down the screen and never moves.

When the two meet, the heading plays to the end on its own.

1898

The Grand Hotel opens on Granby Street, Leicester. A coaching inn was knocked down to make room for it.

1902

It is sold for £150,000. People call it the finest hotel in the Midlands.

1909

Winston Churchill stays here.

1915

A 700-seat cinema opens upstairs, in the ballroom. It closes in 1928.

2009

The hotel is a Ramada. Under it: an army surplus store and an acupuncture clinic.

2024

The shopfronts are rebuilt in wood and glass, as close to the 1898 design as possible.

End of the demo. Scroll back up and watch it again.

The story is true. Sources: Story of Leicester, Wikipedia, Cinema Treasures, Leicester City Council.
