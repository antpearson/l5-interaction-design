3 Try the values

Change the values in the box at the top of the screen. The markers move straight away.

The first value of each pair moves the marker on the heading (start, end). The second moves the line on the screen (scroller-start, scroller-end).

end only makes a difference when scrub is true.

1898

The Grand Hotel opens on Granby Street, Leicester. A coaching inn was knocked down to make room for it.

1902

It is sold for £150,000. People call it the finest hotel in the Midlands.

1909

Winston Churchill stays here.

1915

A 700-seat cinema opens upstairs, in the ballroom. It closes in 1928.

2009

The hotel is a Ramada. Under it: an army surplus store and an acupuncture clinic.

2024

The shopfronts are rebuilt in wood and glass, as close to the 1898 design as possible.
