Click demos

Six demos: one for each kind of interaction in the brief, plus a pop-up and a bar you drag. Every one is the same recipe: a button (or something to grab), a listener, and a change.

  1. Hotspot: click a year on the hotel and its note appears. Click again and it goes. No GSAP.
  2. Toggle: a click plays an animation, another plays it back. Last week's gsap.from() with paused: true.
  3. Choice: two buttons, two outcomes. Each shows its own outcome and hides the other.
  4. Pop-up: a <dialog> that opens over the page, and a <details> fold-out that needs no script. No GSAP.
  5. Slider: slide to compare 1903 and 2009. An <input type="range"> that listens for "input". No GSAP.
  6. Drag the bar: grab the bar on the picture and drag it. Three listeners: press, move, let go. The slider stays for the keyboard. No GSAP.

Each demo is a folder laid out like your own project: index.html, css/style.css, js/main.js and, where it needs them, images. Open its index.html in a browser and read the three files in VS Code.

Demos 2 and 3 need an internet connection: GSAP loads from jsDelivr.
