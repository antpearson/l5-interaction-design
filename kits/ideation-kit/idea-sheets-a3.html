Interaction Design · Week 2 · Round one

Change over time

Something that changed, or a story that unfolded. Write a line or two under each prompt that sparks something; skip the rest. At least six, from either list.

Name

For example, for the first prompt: The bank on the high street. It’s a bar now.

From your own life

  1. A building near you that used to be something else.

  2. A shop, café or venue you used to go to that has closed or changed.

  3. A field, wood or patch of land near you that has been built on.

  4. A park, river, beach or green space near you that has changed.

  5. Something about where you grew up that's different from when you were small.

  6. A job that a machine or an app now does.

  7. A music, gaming or online scene you're part of that has changed since you got into it.

  8. An app, website or piece of tech everyone used that has disappeared.

  9. Something you buy that costs a lot more than it used to.

From the wider world

  1. A person, famous or local, with a life story worth telling.

  2. An expedition, voyage or journey that you find interesting.

  3. A race, rivalry or record attempt.

  4. A disaster, rescue or escape.

  5. A mystery that was never fully solved.

  6. A moment in history you'd like to see from the inside.

  7. A town, coastline or landscape that looks completely different from a hundred years ago.

  8. A glacier, forest or river that has shrunk or grown.

  9. An animal, plant or habitat that is disappearing, or coming back.

  10. An invention that changed how people live.

  11. A band, brand, club or company that rose and fell.

Interaction Design · Week 2 · Round two

Explainers

How something works, or how to do it. Same again: a line or two under each prompt that sparks something, at least six.

Name

For example, for the first prompt: Bouldering. It’s mostly footwork, not arm strength.

From your own life

  1. A hobby or skill that you'd like to educate people on.

  2. Something you know more about than anyone else in this room.

  3. Something about a job you've done that customers never see.

  4. A place you've seen behind the scenes: a kitchen, warehouse, stage or stadium.

  5. The rules of a sport or game that newcomers might find confusing.

  6. An interesting dish you cook that involves a number of steps.

  7. Something you collect that most people wouldn't know about.

From the wider world

  1. Something you've always wondered how it works.

  2. Something you use every day without knowing how it works, like a contactless card or a fridge.

  3. How an instrument or piece of kit makes its sound, like an electric guitar pickup.

  4. What a production role actually involves, like a cinematographer.

  5. A practical skill done properly, like sharpening a knife.

  6. How something gets made, from raw material to shop shelf.

  7. How something is built: a bridge, a ship, a stage set.

  8. A place you'd like to walk someone through: a cave, a ship, a castle, a mine.

  9. How a big system works behind the scenes: the power grid, the post, a football transfer.

  10. How a meme or video takes off and spreads.

  11. Why something happens: tides, the northern lights, a traffic jam with no obvious cause.

  12. How an animal or plant does something remarkable.
