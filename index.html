Interaction Design

Scroll-driven storytelling: everything you are given this term, in one place.

Every session page lives here, along with the kits you will be sent to during class.

Pages go up at least 48 hours before the session they belong to, and they stay up. Read the week before you arrive if you can; come back to it afterwards, because the tasks are written to be worked through again on your own machine. Nothing here is a transcript of the studio. It is the version you can take away.

If a link is dead or a page will not open, tell me rather than working around it.

Sessions

  • Introduction to the module Week 1

    What this module is, why it is not last year with harder code, and getting your machine ready.
  • Before anything moves Week 2

    Your site back on a new Mac, a chapter laid out in Flexbox, a scene that holds still, both built by you, and a long list of story ideas.
  • Finding your story Week 3

    Four shapes a scrolling story can take, archives to raid, and the pitch gate. Nobody leaves without a topic.
  • Storyboarding motion Week 4

    Your story becomes sections, a beat sheet and a drawn storyboard saying what moves and when.
  • From concept to skeleton Week 5 · Component 1 due

    A peer show of concepts, then your storyboard becomes real HTML sections.

Kits

Short, self-contained pages you are pointed at from a session. Each one is worth keeping open in a second tab while you work.

  • Working with GitHub Week 1 onwards · reference

    Set up once, then clone, commit, sync and check, on whichever Mac you land at. Screenshots of every step.
  • Researching and verifying your story Weeks 3 and 5 · directed task

    Sources, verification, image rights, and keeping a record that survives to January.
  • Ideation kit Week 3

    The prompts and constraints used in the topic workshop, so you can run it again alone.
  • Storyboard kit Week 4 · prints onto A4

    Frames for drawing what moves, what holds still, and where the reader gets to act.
  • Section skeleton Week 5 · worked example

    A complete, commented section you can read, copy and adapt to your own story.

The starter project

interaction-design-starter.zip is the Week 1 download, unzipped onto your own machine. That copy is the one you build on all term. If you want to look at it without downloading anything, it is also readable here as interaction-design-starter.

The AI-use log

Your AI-use log, ai-use-log.docx, is in the starter project. A spare blank copy is here. Keep one log for the whole module, add a row on the day whenever you use an AI tool, and upload it with each component, even if it is empty. The rules on what AI may be used for are in section 9 of the assignment brief.
